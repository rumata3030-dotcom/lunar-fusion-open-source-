
### 1. Space Power Grid (Echelon 1)
*   **Orbital Solar Farms:** 4 ultra-lightweight metallized Mylar solar concentrators deployed in high stable lunar orbits, ensuring 24/7 solar capture.
*   **Satellite-Dispatchers:** A low-orbit constellation (100 km altitude, 118-min period) acting as dynamic laser optical locks. They ingest solar energy from the farms and beam a **13.8 MW Infrared Laser (1064 nm)** directly onto the harvester on the surface.
*   **Laser Damping:** The 0.05 N photon pressure recoil is utilized by the satellite's onboard AI to passively compensate for lunar gravitational anomalies (mascons), saving ion-thruster propellant.

### 2. Autonomous Lunar Harvester & VKGR (Echelon 2)
*   **Vertical Coaxial-Gravitational Recuperator (VKGR):** A 2.5-meter vertical chamber constructed from High-Density Silicon Carbide (SiC). Eliminates mechanical augers or screws to prevent abrasive wear from razor-sharp lunar regolith.
*   **Ultrasound Fluidization:** Integrated piezo-actuators operating at **22–25 kHz** induce a "fluidized bed" state in the regolith, dropping internal friction to zero.
*   **Thermodynamic Loop:** Cold regolith is pumped upward through the core. At the top rim, the orbital laser flashes it to **+700°C** to desorb $^3\text{He}$. The spent regolith spills over the rim, cascading down the outer sleeve under lunar gravity, preheating the incoming cold core to **+428°C** with a **60% thermal recuperation efficiency**.
*   **Storage Capacity:** Features an 8.5m wide-track automated rover frame packing a **25 m³ cryogenic storage vault** (16.2 m³ liquid $H_2$, 4.6 m³ liquid $He$, and 0.17 m³ liquid $O_2$).

### 3. Deep Space Logistics (Echelon 3)
*   **Orbital Fusion Vessel "Selena-Prime":** A 130-meter structural truss ship powered by a linear **Direct Fusion Drive (DFD)** using $D+^3\text{He}$ fuel. Expels lunar hydrogen mass at **150 km/s**, generating **573 N of continuous thrust** to reach Jupiter (Europa) in **80 days**. Features a 20m centrifuge providing **1g artificial gravity**.
*   **Interplanetary Shuttle "Hermes":** A high-thrust vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) lander running on free byproduct fuel ($LH_2/LOX$).
*   **The Bridge Integration (The Core Feature):** The docking interface of *Hermes* is built rigidly on its aft frame, positioned squarely **between its chemical rocket bells**. When docked to the bow of *Selena-Prime*, *Hermes* acts as the **Integrated Command Bridge**. Its chemical thrusters serve as high-leverage nose attitude control thrusters and actively damp out micro-vibrations generated by the living quarters' gravity centrifuge.
