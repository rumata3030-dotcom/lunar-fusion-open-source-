# Author Background: From Waste Oil to Lunar Regolith

Before this lunar project, I spent two years building a waste-oil-to-diesel reactor as a hobby.

That reactor taught me the hardest lesson in thermal engineering: heating a poorly-conducting medium evenly, without coking or sintering, is the real problem — not the chemistry.

Lunar ³He extraction has the exact same physics problem: heating regolith to 700°C without sintering it, recovering most of the heat, and keeping the walls clean.

This repository is my attempt to transfer that practical experience to the Moon. It is deliberately raw and optimistic — if the idea is worth anything, smarter people will find the flaws and fix them. That is the point.
